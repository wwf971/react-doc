## What they are for

A link is written once, in the card that needs another; the box works out the other direction. At the foot of a card, **Pointed at by** lists the cards whose text links here, with the sentence around the link.

Questions use the same mechanism per option: a card lists the questions that refer to it, and which options do ([Q2](q002.md)).
