## What they are for

A link is written once, in the card that needs another; the box works out the other direction. The connections of a zettel show them on its left, as **Back links**: the zettels whose text links here.

Questions use the same mechanism per option: a card lists the questions that refer to it, and which options do ([Q2](q002.md)).
