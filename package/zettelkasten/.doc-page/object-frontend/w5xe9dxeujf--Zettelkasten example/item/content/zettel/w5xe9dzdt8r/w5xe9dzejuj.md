## What a card holds

One card, one idea ([One card, one idea](card-atomic.md)), in your own words ([Written in your own words](card-own-words.md)). A card has a title that says the idea and a summary that stands alone, since both are what other cards and the map show of it.

<!--renderComp=Anchor,id=kind-->

## Kinds of cards

| Kind | Holds |
| --- | --- |
| knowledge card | an idea, a fact, a rule |
| question card | a question with its options, and why each is right or wrong |
| note | your own remark on a card or a place of it |
