## Levels

This box has three levels below its root: areas, topics and facts. The level says how much a card covers, and a card names the card above it ([Hub cards](structure-hub.md)). The connections of a zettel draw this hierarchy above and below it; the links cut across it, on its sides. A zettel may be held by more than one ([Hub cards](structure-hub.md) is held by Structure and by Links), never by one it holds: containment has no cycles.
