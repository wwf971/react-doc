## The rule

<!--renderComp=Anchor,id=whole-->

Write a card so that another card can point at it and mean all of it.

A card that covers two ideas gets links that mean only one of them, and the reader arriving through such a link has to search. Split it, and let the two cards link each other ([Links](link.md)).

<!--renderComp=Anchor,id=test-->

## A test

Can you give the card a title that is a statement, not a topic? "Semantic chunking keeps sentences together" is a card; "Chunking" is a place where cards hang ([Hub cards](structure-hub.md)).
